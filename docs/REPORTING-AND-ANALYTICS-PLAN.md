# REPORTING & ANALYTICS PLAN: Business Intelligence Engine
**The Petal & Bloom Commerce Operating System**
**Role:** Business Operations Consultant & Analytics Architect

---

## 1. Reporting Philosophy: Questions Over Vanity Metrics

A luxury artisan floral business fails when the founder drowns in meaningless vanity metrics (page views, clicks) while missing critical cash-flow and operational bottlenecks. 

The Petal & Bloom BI engine transforms raw database rows into **answers to urgent business questions**:

| Business Question | Raw Table Source | Actionable Operational Answer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"How much actual cash did we bank this week?"* | `orders`, `payments` | **Net Revenue:** Gross Sales minus Coupon Discounts, Petal Point Redemptions, and Refunds. |
| *"Which flowers are driving our business?"* | `order_items` | **Product Velocity:** Top 5 arrangements by margin and units crafted. |
| *"Are we fulfilling on schedule?"* | `order_status_history` | **Artisan SLA:** Average hours elapsed between `PAYMENT_CONFIRMED` and `PACKED`. |
| *"What is our loyalty debt?"* | `loyalty_accounts` | **Outstanding Liability:** Total unredeemed Petal Points in circulation converted to INR. |
| *"Are influencers generating profitable sales?"* | `coupons`, `orders` | **Affiliate ROI:** Net GMV generated per influencer code compared to commission due. |

## 2. The 6 Core Operational Reports

### Report 1: Financial & Cash Reconciliation Report
* **Target Audience:** Founder / Chartered Accountant.
* **Cadence:** Weekly & Monthly.
* **Metrics Computed:**
  * **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV):** $\sum \text{subtotal\_in\_paise} / 100$
  * **Promotional Discounts Given:** $\sum \text{discount\_in\_paise} / 100$
  * **Loyalty Points Redeemed:** $\sum \text{loyalty\_discount\_in\_paise} / 100$
  * **Shipping Fees Collected:** $\sum \text{shipping\_fee\_in\_paise} / 100$
  * **Refunds Issued:** $\sum \text{payments.amount\_in\_paise} / 100 \quad (\text{where status} = \text{'REFUNDED'})$
  * **Net Banked Sales:** $\text{GMV} - \text{Discounts} - \text{Loyalty} - \text{Refunds} + \text{Shipping}$
* **Cashfree Reconciliation:** Compares gateway settlements against internal orders to identify uncaptured sessions or payment mismatches.

---

### Report 2: Studio Crafting & Product Velocity Report
* **Target Audience:** Studio Fulfillment Lead.
* **Cadence:** Daily / Weekly.
* **Metrics Computed:**
  * Units crafted per arrangement.
  * Most popular yarn shades (e.g., 62% of roses ordered in "Dusty Rose", 28% in "Ruby Red").
  * Add-on attach rate: % of orders choosing premium gift wrapping (₹79) or personalized greeting card (₹39).
  * Out-of-stock frequency and made-to-order turnaround days.

---

### Report 3: Patron Cohort & Retention Analysis
* **Target Audience:** Marketing Lead.
* **Cadence:** Monthly.
* **Metrics Computed:**
  * **New vs. Repeat Customers:** Ratio of first-time buyers to multi-order patrons.
  * **Repeat Purchase Interval:** Average days between Order 1 and Order 2.
  * **Cohort LTV Curve:** 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day cumulative spend per registration cohort.
  * **Heirloom VIP Concentration:** Percentage of total GMV contributed by the top 10% of patrons.

---

### Report 4: Loyalty Program & Liability Report
* **Target Audience:** Finance / Founder.
* **Cadence:** Monthly.
* **Metrics Computed:**
  * Points issued (via welcome gift, purchases, and referrals).
  * Points redeemed at checkout (burn rate).
  * Points expired / forfeited.
  * **Current Balance Liability:** Total unredeemed points $\times$ ₹1.00 INR sitting on the balance sheet.
  * Break-even ROI: Revenue generated by repeat orders utilizing loyalty points vs. discount expense.

---

### Report 5: Influencer & Campaign Attribution Report
* **Target Audience:** Marketing Lead.
* **Cadence:** Weekly.
* **Metrics Computed:**
  * Redemptions per influencer coupon code.
  * Attributed gross and net revenue.
  * Average Order Value per campaign.
  * Commission payable calculation based on configured `commission_percent`.

---

### Report 6: Logistics Partner Performance Report
* **Target Audience:** Operations Lead.
* **Cadence:** Weekly.
* **Metrics Computed:**
  * Dispatch SLA: Time from order placement to courier handover.
  * Transit SLA: Average days from pickup to delivery by zone (Metro vs. Tier-2 vs. Rest of India).
  * RTO Rate: Return-to-origin percentage by carrier (`DELHIVERY` vs. `SHIPROCKET` vs. `INDIA_POST`).
  * Exception Rate: Parcels delayed, damaged, or marked "door locked".

---

## 3. Data Extraction & Delivery Strategy

1. **In-App Visual Dashboards:** Filterable charts and aggregated metric cards rendered directly in the admin portal (`/admin/dashboard` and `/admin/reports`).
2. **RFC 4180 CSV Export:** Direct download button on every report table allowing deep analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.
3. **Automated Monday Morning Digest:** (Phase 3) A scheduled Vercel cron triggers an email summary to the founder with the prior week's GMV, order volume, top piece, and active loyalty liability.
